03 · MISTAKES

Three ways to ruin this build

Each one is a real first-build classic. Here they cost nothing, so make them on purpose.

Step 02

Skip the thermal paste

Metal on metal looks flat but is full of microscopic air gaps, and air is an insulator. The chip runs to its 95 °C limit in seconds.

Step 04

RAM side by side

Two sticks in A1 + A2 share one memory channel. The PC boots and nothing warns you. It is just slower, forever.

Step 07

Forget the GPU cable

The slot alone gives a graphics card 75 W. This one needs about 220 W. Fans spin, lights glow, the screen stays black.
